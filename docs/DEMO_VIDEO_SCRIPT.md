# ScreenSense Demo Video Script

Target length: 2-3 minutes.

1. **Introduction (15 s):** Show the tray icon and introduce ScreenSense as a local,
   optional eye-wellness reminder app, not a medical product.
2. **Onboarding (25 s):** Walk through the privacy promise and stored-data explanation.
   Show that camera monitoring is optional and off by default.
3. **Break experience (35 s):** Open the tray menu, start Eye Yoga, show one exercise,
   pause, and exit to demonstrate user control.
4. **Context and insights (30 s):** Open the dashboard and point out score, timeline,
   trend, streak, and the export/clear-history controls.
5. **Accessibility and preferences (25 s):** Switch between light/dark themes, turn on
   reduced motion, and show the autostart preference.
6. **Optional camera control (25 s):** Only if tested with explicit consent, show the
   camera-active tray indicator and stop action. Do not record identifiable camera
   footage or display frame data.
7. **Close (10 s):** Reiterate that stored data stays on-device and show the
   non-medical disclaimer.
